## What funding would finish

The user decides whether to use a draft and personally submits any filing. Monthly access, if approved, is for software tools and education; no consumer outcome is promised.

## Kickstarter eligibility must be resolved first

[Kickstarter's rules](https://www.kickstarter.com/rules) require compliance with its payment partner's rules. [Stripe's prohibited-business list](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses) includes credit repair and counseling services. This creates a material eligibility question for this product. This is an inference about possible classification, **not a finding that Kickstarter has rejected CreditRepairAI**.

Provide the platform with the actual features, AI-guided drafts, proposed pricing and marketing. Request a written eligibility determination before investing in a Kickstarter launch. Calling the app software does not by itself establish eligibility. Do not conceal credit-related features or use another business classification to bypass the rules. Funding materials should contain no deletion, score, approval or outcome-date claims. Software crowdfunding and AI-created promotional materials also need to satisfy Kickstarter's current disclosure rules.
