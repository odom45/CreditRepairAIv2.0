# Federal FCRA case-law scope

Reviewed: 2026-09-16

The case-law layer supplies official opinion text plus a narrow, reviewable synopsis. It is secondary to the current statute and regulation and does not turn the agent into a lawyer or a litigation service.

## Permitted use

- Explain a published holding at a high level after retrieving the official opinion.
- Identify whether the decision is Supreme Court authority or circuit-specific precedent.
- Use the decision to improve factual questions, evidence checklists, and dispute precision when the verified case facts materially match.
- Tell the user when an asserted error appears objectively and readily verifiable versus dependent on unresolved legal interpretation.

## Prohibited use until attorney review

- Do not predict suit viability, liability, damages, limitation periods, attorney fees, venue, preemption, or appeal outcomes.
- Do not say a circuit case controls a user outside that circuit.
- Do not infer a holding from a party's allegation, dissent, concurrence, dicta, or lower-court history.
- Do not use case law to encourage a false dispute, a false identity-theft claim, or removal of accurate current information.
- Do not treat a lack of discovered negative treatment as a formal citator result.

`OFFICIAL_TEXT_VERIFIED` means the cited opinion text was checked against an official court, GovInfo, Supreme Court, or Library of Congress source. `ATTORNEY_REVIEW_PENDING` means later history and litigation use have not received the deferred attorney/citator review. The agent may explain the holding for educational planning but must disclose the scope and must escalate litigation conclusions.
